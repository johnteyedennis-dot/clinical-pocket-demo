Dr. Sherri's SmartNotes Clinical Flow

1. Content → 2. AI Prompt → 3. Clear Notes → 4. Clinical Thinking → 5. Active Recall
🔍

In nursing school, it often feels like you have to memorize everything—every symptom, every medication, and every detail.

Core Principle: Memorizing is not how nurses think, and it is not how you build clinical judgment.

SmartNotes help you turn overwhelming content into clear, organized, high-yield notes that actually make sense. You learn what matters and why it matters for patient care.

You take the content you need to study, run it through a guided prompt, and receive organized notes you can use to think, review, and quiz yourself with intention:

  1. 1 Content: Gather your raw lecture slides, textbook pages, or disease topic.
  2. 2 AI Prompt: Run the material through the structured SmartNotes prompt.
  3. 3 Clear Notes: Generate clean, high-yield structured summaries.
  4. 4 Clinical Thinking: Connect symptoms and interventions to real bedside priorities.
  5. 5 Active Recall: Quiz yourself intentionally instead of passively rereading.

1. Compress the content

• AI helps pull out the most important, testable ideas without the fluff.

2. Organize the information

• You get clear visual structure instead of overwhelming lecture chaos.

3. Add clinical judgment

• You learn what matters most in a real patient situation.
