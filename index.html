Cardiopulmonary Quick-Guide

pH: 7.35 – 7.45 (Acidemia < 7.35 | Alkalemia > 7.45)

PaCO2: 35 – 45 mmHg (Respiratory component)

HCO3-: 22 – 26 mEq/L (Metabolic component)

PaO2: 80 – 100 mmHg on room air

Fine Crackles: High-pitched popping at end-inspiration (Pulmonary fibrosis, early CHF).

Wheezes: High-pitched musical sounds from narrowed airways (Asthma, bronchospasm).

Stridor: High-pitched monophonic upper airway sound (Croup, post-extubation edema).

Albuterol (Salbutamol): Beta-2 agonist. Onset: 5–15 min. Relaxes bronchial smooth muscle.

Ipratropium Bromide: Anticholinergic (SAMA). Blocks muscarinic receptors to prevent bronchoconstriction.
